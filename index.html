HOW TO PLAY
Left joystick moves your soldier. Drag the right side of the screen to look around.
Tap FIRE to shoot, hold AIM to steady your aim for better accuracy.
Sprint then tap CROUCH to slide into cover or tackle an enemy's legs.
Every elimination randomly swaps your weapon — stay adaptable!
First team to 10 points across 5 rounds wins the match.
